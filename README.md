# My Favorite Movie: Interstellar

## Introduction

**Interstellar** is my favorite science fiction movie. It was directed by Christopher Nolan and released in 2014.

## About the Movie

*Interstellar* is about a former pilot named Cooper who joins a space mission to find a new habitable planet for humanity. As Earth faces environmental problems, Cooper and his team travel through space in search of a new home.

> "Love is the one thing that transcends time and space."

## My Favorite Part

My favorite part is when Cooper enters the black hole and discovers the connection between time, gravity, and his daughter Murphy.
